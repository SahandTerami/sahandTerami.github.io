Reservoir Computing as a Nonlinear Transfer Function Framework

  • Collaborators: Andrew Flynn, Francesco Sorrentino
  • September 2026 - Present

Motivation

Characterizing transfer functions for nonlinear systems is challenging due to their complex and input-dependent input-output relationships. In this research, we aim to develop a reservoir computing framework that learns a generalized transfer function for nonlinear systems and can represent their dynamics across diverse and previously unseen input signals.
